# Presets

Factory programs (the bar and the host program list):

1. **Body** — two close resonators, light mix.
2. **Minor Chord** — root, minor third, fifth, octave.
3. **Major Chord** — the major version.
4. **Metallic** — inharmonic offsets, more intensity.
5. **Drone** — long decay, low root.
6. **Narrow Laser** — one tight peak.
7. **Wide Pad** — spread pans and detune.
8. **Octaves** — stacked octaves.
9. **Harmonics** — partials of a low root.
10. **Init** — one voice at the root.
